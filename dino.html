Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Dino-Trex: Mini Oyunlar (also referred to as Dino-Trex: Mini Games, and previously published as Block Puzzle & Snake Games), developed and published on Google Play by Adorsoft ("we", "us", or "our").

The app includes casual games such as block puzzles, snake, a dinosaur runner, and other mini games. This policy explains how information may be processed when you use the app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store game progress, scores, coins, achievements, preferences, and settings locally on your device to provide gameplay and remember your choices.

We do not operate an account system or a separate user database for the app. Local game data is not uploaded to our own servers. Depending on your device and backup settings, some app data may be included in a device backup managed by your operating system.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and includes Unity Ads mediation. Depending on the ads served, Google, Unity, and their partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data.

These providers may use information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, improve their services, and personalize ads where permitted by law and applicable settings.

For more information about these providers, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Unity Privacy Policy

3. Data Sharing

Third-party advertising providers and their partners may receive or process information as described above and in their own privacy policies. We do not operate a separate account system through which users submit personal information to us.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings are generally stored on your device for as long as the app data remains on the device. You can remove local app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app. A device backup, if one exists, may need to be managed separately through your device or backup provider.

The app does not provide user accounts, so there is no app account to delete. Advertising providers may retain information under their own policies. Please refer to the links above for information about their data practices and available privacy choices.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Please keep your device and operating system up to date and use the security features available on your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.
